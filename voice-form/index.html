Citizen Privacy & Security Commitment

Your voice data stays strictly under your control.

  • No Server Audio Storage: Voice recognition runs via your browser's standard Web Speech interface. Raw audio stream is never recorded, saved, or shared.
  • Local Parsing: Spoken conversions (email symbols, phone digits, dates) are interpreted strictly on your device using client-side JavaScript.
  • Zero Secret Keys Exposed: Prototype requires no paid external credentials or third-party tracking scripts.
